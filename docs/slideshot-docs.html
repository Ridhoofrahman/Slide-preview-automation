OverviewRingkasan

Slideshot connects designers and Design Managers (DMs) while an order is being made. Designers send finished slides for review from inside PowerPoint. DMs hand out the work, review every slide, and see when an order is ready for the customer.Slideshot menghubungkan designer dan Design Manager (DM) selama sebuah order dikerjakan. Designer mengirim slide yang selesai untuk direview langsung dari PowerPoint. DM membagi pekerjaan, mereview setiap slide, dan melihat kapan order siap untuk customer.

Getting startedMemulai

Install the add-inMemasang add-in

  • Mac: put manifest.xml in ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Powerpoint/Data/Documents/wef, restart PowerPoint, then open Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins and pick Slideshot.Mac: taruh manifest.xml di ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Powerpoint/Data/Documents/wef, restart PowerPoint, lalu buka Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins dan pilih Slideshot.
  • Windows: add the shared folder that holds manifest.xml as an add-in catalog, then open Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins → Shared Folder.Windows: tambahkan shared folder yang berisi manifest.xml sebagai katalog add-in, lalu buka Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins → Shared Folder.
  • Ask your admin for the current manifest file.Minta file manifest terbaru ke admin.
Slideshot needs a recent Microsoft 365 PowerPoint that can capture slide images. On older versions the panel says “This PowerPoint version can’t capture slide images.”Slideshot butuh PowerPoint Microsoft 365 versi terbaru yang bisa mengambil gambar slide. Di versi lama, panel menampilkan “This PowerPoint version can’t capture slide images.”

Sign in the first timeMasuk pertama kali

  1. Enter your email and password.Masukkan email dan password.
  2. Choose Your team: Avvenger, Cascade, Clementine, Trident or Magnetic.Pilih Your team: Avvenger, Cascade, Clementine, Trident, atau Magnetic.
  3. Type your name the way you want your DM or designers to see it.Ketik namamu seperti yang ingin dilihat DM atau designer.

Your team and name are saved to your account, so you only enter them once. The same sign-in works on the Dashboard.Tim dan namamu tersimpan di akun, jadi cukup diisi sekali. Sign-in yang sama berlaku di Dashboard.

For designersUntuk designer

Slides can only be sent once your DM has distributed the order. If you haven’t been given slides yet, everything in the panel shows “Not to work”. Ask your DM first.Slide hanya bisa dikirim setelah DM membagi order. Jika kamu belum diberi slide, semua slide di panel menampilkan “Not to work”. Tanyakan ke DM terlebih dahulu.

1. Tell Slideshot which order this is1. Beri tahu Slideshot ini order yang mana

  1. Open the deck and open Slideshot. The first screen asks Which order is this?Buka deck dan buka Slideshot. Layar pertama menanyakan Which order is this?
  2. Type the Order ID exactly as shown on the order page in the Inner Platform. Digits are enough; the # is added automatically.Ketik Order ID persis seperti di halaman order di Inner Platform. Cukup angka; tanda # ditambahkan otomatis.
  3. Click Continue.Klik Continue.
  • If the order hasn’t been distributed, you’ll see “We can’t find this order…”. Ask your DM to assign slides first.Jika order belum dibagi, muncul “We can’t find this order…”. Minta DM membagi slide terlebih dahulu.
  • This is asked once per deck and saved inside the file. You can still change the Order ID from the header later.Ini ditanyakan sekali per deck dan tersimpan di dalam file. Order ID tetap bisa diubah dari header nanti.

2. Know your panel2. Kenali panelnya

3. Send a slide for review3. Kirim slide untuk review

  1. Select the slide in PowerPoint. The panel shows its tag and assignment.Pilih slide di PowerPoint. Panel menampilkan tag dan assignment-nya.
  2. Optional: click the pen button to add a note for your DM, for example “Used the client’s new icon set”.Opsional: klik tombol pena untuk menambah catatan untuk DM, misalnya “Used the client’s new icon set”.
  3. Click Mark slide N ready for review.Klik Mark slide N ready for review.

The DM receives a picture of the slide, its fonts, size and speaker notes, your note, and your name and team. The tag changes to Waiting for review and a badge such as “v1 sent, 5 min ago” appears on the preview.DM menerima gambar slide, font, ukuran dan speaker notes-nya, catatanmu, serta nama dan timmu. Tag berubah menjadi Waiting for review dan lencana seperti “v1 sent, 5 min ago” muncul di preview.

4. Upload a newer version4. Unggah versi terbaru

  1. After the DM asks for changes (or you improved the slide), edit it in PowerPoint.Setelah DM meminta perubahan (atau kamu memperbaiki slide), edit di PowerPoint.
  2. Select the slide and click Upload the latest version.Pilih slide lalu klik Upload the latest version.
  3. Click Send v2 for review (the number goes up each time).Klik Send v2 for review (angkanya naik setiap kali).

Older versions stay on the Dashboard, read-only, so your DM can compare.Versi lama tetap ada di Dashboard, hanya-baca, sehingga DM bisa membandingkan.

5. Change a slide’s position5. Ubah posisi slide

Use this when the slide in your file should appear at a different place in the order. For example, your file’s slide 9 should be shown to the DM as slide 4.Pakai ini jika slide di fileku seharusnya muncul di tempat lain di order. Misalnya slide 9 di filemu harus dilihat DM sebagai slide 4.

  1. Select the slide, then click the left button (Change slide position).Pilih slide, lalu klik tombol kiri (Change slide position).
  2. Current position shows where the slide is in your file. Under Sent as, choose the position it should have in the order.Current position menunjukkan posisi slide di filemu. Di Sent as, pilih posisinya di order.
  3. Click Save. The header now reads “Slide 9 → Slide 4”, which is exactly what your DM will see.Klik Save. Header kini menampilkan “Slide 9 → Slide 4”, persis seperti yang akan dilihat DM.
  • Positions already taken by another slide that was sent are greyed out (“Slide 4 · sent”), so two slides can never end up at the same number.Posisi yang sudah dipakai slide lain yang terkirim berwarna abu-abu (“Slide 4 · sent”), jadi dua slide tidak akan pernah memiliki nomor yang sama.
  • If the position you pick is held by another slide in your file that hasn’t been sent, the two slides swap places.Jika posisi yang kamu pilih dipegang slide lain di filemu yang belum dikirim, kedua slide bertukar tempat.
  • Already-sent slides can move too. The new position applies from the next version you send.Slide yang sudah dikirim juga bisa dipindah. Posisi baru berlaku mulai versi berikutnya yang kamu kirim.
  • If your DM gave you specific slides, you can only choose among those positions.Jika DM memberimu slide tertentu, kamu hanya bisa memilih di antara posisi itu.

6. Working in a new file6. Mengerjakan di file baru

Many designers build their slides in a fresh presentation instead of the original deck. That works. When you type the Order ID, Slideshot notices that this file’s slides don’t match the order’s deck and shows a purple note: Is this your working file for #12345? Click Continue again to proceed.Banyak designer membuat slide di presentasi baru, bukan di deck aslinya. Itu boleh. Saat kamu mengetik Order ID, Slideshot mengenali bahwa slide di file ini tidak sama dengan deck order dan menampilkan catatan ungu: Is this your working file for #12345? Klik Continue sekali lagi untuk lanjut.

  • Your slides are placed, in order, into the positions your DM assigned you. If your DM gave you slides 6–10, your file’s slides 1–5 go to 6–10.Slide-mu ditempatkan, berurutan, ke posisi yang ditugaskan DM. Jika DM memberimu slide 6–10, slide 1–5 di filemu masuk ke 6–10.
  • Use Change slide position to correct any of them.Pakai Change slide position untuk mengoreksinya.

7. When a slide says “Not to work”7. Ketika slide bertuliskan “Not to work”

The slide wasn’t assigned to anyone, or the order hasn’t been distributed. The panel hides all buttons for these slides so nothing is sent by mistake. Slides assigned to someone else show “Assigned to Name-Team” and are also read-only for you.Slide itu tidak ditugaskan ke siapa pun, atau order belum dibagi. Panel menyembunyikan semua tombol di slide ini agar tidak ada yang terkirim secara tidak sengaja. Slide yang ditugaskan ke orang lain menampilkan “Assigned to Name-Team” dan juga hanya-baca untukmu.

8. “Order ID has already been used”8. “Order ID has already been used”

Shown when a different deck already uses this Order ID and you aren’t assigned slides in it. Check the number, or type a different one in the header. Copies of the same deck, and working files for orders you’re assigned to, don’t trigger this.Muncul jika deck lain sudah memakai Order ID ini dan kamu tidak ditugaskan slide di dalamnya. Periksa nomornya, atau ketik nomor lain di header. Salinan deck yang sama, dan working file untuk order yang ditugaskan kepadamu, tidak memicu pesan ini.

  1. Click Assign slides in the board header, in a card’s ⋯ menu, or Distribute project on an empty board.Klik Assign slides di header board, di menu ⋯ kartu, atau Distribute project di board kosong.
  2. Enter the order number.Masukkan nomor order.
  3. Pick a designer. The list is grouped into Your team and Other teams. You can assign slides to yourself too.Pilih designer. Daftarnya dikelompokkan menjadi Your team dan Other teams. Kamu juga bisa menugaskan slide ke dirimu sendiri.

Send to customerSend to customer

When every sent slide is approved, a Send to customer button appears in the header. Click it to mark the order as sent; the ring turns solid green. You can also use Mark as sent to customer, and Undo, from the card menu. Progress counts sent slides only, so a slide that hasn’t been sent yet doesn’t hold the order back.Jika semua slide yang terkirim sudah approved, tombol Send to customer muncul di header. Klik untuk menandai order terkirim; ring menjadi hijau solid. Kamu juga bisa memakai Mark as sent to customer, dan Undo, dari menu kartu. Progres hanya menghitung slide yang terkirim, jadi slide yang belum dikirim tidak menahan order.

This marks the order’s status in Slideshot. It doesn’t email or deliver anything to the customer yet.Ini menandai status order di Slideshot. Belum mengirim email atau mengirimkan apa pun ke customer.

Admin and setupAdmin dan setup

Slideshot has three pieces: the add-in and the Dashboard (static pages served from GitHub Pages) and a Supabase project that holds accounts, the database, private slide-image storage and live updates.Slideshot punya tiga bagian: add-in dan Dashboard (halaman statis dari GitHub Pages) serta proyek Supabase yang menyimpan akun, database, penyimpanan gambar slide yang privat, dan pembaruan langsung.

Add or remove a DMMenambah atau menghapus DM

DMs are listed in the design_managers table. Run this in the Supabase SQL Editor, with the person’s sign-in email:DM tercatat di tabel design_managers. Jalankan ini di Supabase SQL Editor, dengan email sign-in orangnya:

insert into public.design_managers (user_id)
select id from auth.users where email = 'dm@24slides.com';

-- remove a DM
delete from public.design_managers
where user_id = (select id from auth.users where email = 'dm@24slides.com');
  • The person must have signed in at least once so their account exists.Orangnya harus sudah pernah sign in agar akunnya ada.
  • Approving, commenting, deleting and distributing are enforced in the database for DMs only, so they can’t be bypassed from the browser.Approve, komentar, hapus, dan membagi slide dibatasi hanya untuk DM di database, jadi tidak bisa dilewati dari browser.

  • Designers see the new version after they close and reopen the panel. The version appears in the panel footer.Designer melihat versi baru setelah menutup dan membuka lagi panel. Versi tampil di footer panel.
  • When the manifest changes (name or icon), replace manifest.xml in the shared folder, close PowerPoint and, on Windows, delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Office\16.0\Wef before reinstalling.Jika manifest berubah (nama atau ikon), ganti manifest.xml di shared folder, tutup PowerPoint dan, di Windows, hapus %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Office\16.0\Wef sebelum memasang ulang.
  • The setup script supabase-setup.sql in the repository creates the tables and rules.Skrip setup supabase-setup.sql di repository membuat tabel dan aturannya.

FAQFAQ

Why do all my slides say “Not to work”?Kenapa semua slide-ku bertuliskan “Not to work”?
Your DM hasn’t distributed this order, or it was distributed under a different number. Check the Order ID in the header and ask your DM to assign you slides.DM belum membagi order ini, atau dibagi dengan nomor yang berbeda. Periksa Order ID di header dan minta DM menugaskan slide kepadamu.
Can I use a new file instead of the original deck?Bolehkah memakai file baru, bukan deck asli?
Yes. Slideshot shows the “working file” note and places your slides into the positions assigned to you. See “Working in a new file”.Boleh. Slideshot menampilkan catatan “working file” dan menempatkan slide-mu ke posisi yang ditugaskan. Lihat “Mengerjakan di file baru”.
Why is a position greyed out in the dropdown?Kenapa ada posisi yang abu-abu di dropdown?
Another slide already sent to that position. Choose a free one, or ask the other designer to move theirs.Slide lain sudah dikirim ke posisi itu. Pilih posisi yang kosong, atau minta designer lain memindahkan miliknya.
Can I move a slide that was already sent?Bisakah memindahkan slide yang sudah dikirim?
Yes. Pick a new position and send the next version; the DM sees it at the new position from then on.Bisa. Pilih posisi baru dan kirim versi berikutnya; DM melihatnya di posisi baru sejak saat itu.
What if I delete a slide and build it again?Bagaimana jika slide dihapus lalu dibuat ulang?
Slideshot tracks slides by PowerPoint’s internal ID, so a rebuilt slide counts as a new slide. The assignment follows the position number, but the review history and versions stay with the old slide. Edit the original when you can.Slideshot melacak slide lewat ID internal PowerPoint, jadi slide yang dibuat ulang dianggap slide baru. Assignment mengikuti nomor posisi, tapi riwayat review dan versi tetap di slide lama. Sebisa mungkin edit slide aslinya.
Can designers open the Dashboard?Bisakah designer membuka Dashboard?
Not yet. It is DM-only today. A designer view is planned.Belum. Saat ini hanya untuk DM. Tampilan untuk designer sudah direncanakan.
Does Send to customer email the customer?Apakah Send to customer mengirim email ke customer?
No. It marks the order as sent inside Slideshot.Tidak. Itu hanya menandai order sebagai terkirim di dalam Slideshot.
How long are orders kept?Berapa lama order disimpan?
Orders are removed automatically 30 days after their last activity. DMs can delete them earlier.Order dihapus otomatis 30 hari setelah aktivitas terakhirnya. DM bisa menghapusnya lebih awal.
How do I change my team or name?Bagaimana mengganti tim atau namaku?
DMs can change their name by clicking it in the Dashboard header. The team is set at first sign-in; ask the admin to change it. Old actions keep the name they were made with.DM bisa mengganti nama dengan mengkliknya di header Dashboard. Tim ditetapkan saat sign-in pertama; minta admin untuk mengubahnya. Aksi lama tetap memakai nama saat aksi itu dibuat.
What does my DM see of my slide?Apa yang dilihat DM dari slide-ku?
A picture of the slide, its fonts, slide size, speaker notes, your note, the version and who sent it.Gambar slide, font, ukuran slide, speaker notes, catatanmu, versi, dan siapa pengirimnya.

Limits and what’s nextBatasan dan rencana

Today’s limitsBatasan saat ini

  • Assignments follow slide numbers. If the original deck’s order changes, update the distribution.Pembagian mengikuti nomor slide. Jika urutan deck asli berubah, perbarui pembagiannya.
  • A rebuilt slide is a new slide: its history doesn’t follow.Slide yang dibuat ulang adalah slide baru: riwayatnya tidak ikut.
  • Send to customer only marks status.Send to customer hanya menandai status.
  • The Dashboard is DM-only.Dashboard hanya untuk DM.
  • Slide images need a recent Microsoft 365 PowerPoint.Gambar slide membutuhkan PowerPoint Microsoft 365 versi terbaru.

PlannedDirencanakan

  • A Dashboard for everyone: designers see their workload, their team and who is working on what.Dashboard untuk semua orang: designer melihat beban kerja, timnya, dan siapa mengerjakan apa.
  • Connecting Send to customer to the Inner Platform (idea).Menghubungkan Send to customer ke Inner Platform (ide).
  • Recognising a replacement slide as the next version of the one it replaced (parked).Mengenali slide pengganti sebagai versi berikutnya dari slide yang digantikan (ditunda).
