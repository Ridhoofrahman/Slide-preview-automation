Compile Lab

Prototype. Split a deck into one file per slide, then compile those files back into one deck.

1

Split this deck into files

Open the deck you want to split, then click the button. Each slide becomes its own .pptx file, saved in this add-in.

    2

    Compile into this presentation

    Open a new blank presentation, open Compile Lab there, then click Compile. Files are put back in slide order, whatever order they arrived in.
